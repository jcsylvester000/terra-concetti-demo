Anilao is a rare thing: a celebrated dive destination you can reach by car. No domestic flight, no early-morning ferry: from Metro Manila the trip is about **122 km and roughly two hours** from NAIA, mostly on expressway. Here is how to plan it, whether you are driving yourself, taking the bus or booking a transfer.

To understand where you are going before you set off, start with our [guide to Anilao](/anilao): the dive sites, the seasons and what is around.

## By car: the fastest way

Most visitors drive, and the route is straightforward.

1. **Metro Manila to SLEX.** Head south onto the South Luzon Expressway (SLEX). From NAIA or Makati this is the natural starting point.
2. **SLEX to the STAR Tollway.** Continue south through Laguna and onto the Southern Tagalog Arterial Road (STAR Tollway) toward Batangas City.
3. **Toward Bauan and Mabini.** Leave the tollway near Batangas City and follow the road west through Bauan to Mabini, on the Calumpang Peninsula.
4. **Along the coast to Anilao.** From Mabini, the Mabini Circumferential Road runs along the coast past dive resorts and small villages to Anilao.

Allow about two hours from NAIA on a clear run. On long weekends and holidays, the expressways south of Manila get busy, so leave early or travel on a weekday if you can.

### Driving tips

- **Toll payments.** SLEX and the STAR Tollway are toll roads. Check that your RFID account is loaded before you leave.
- **Fuel and stops.** There are service areas along SLEX; fill up before the provincial roads.
- **Coastal roads.** The last stretch is narrower and winding, with tricycles and pedestrians. Take it slowly, especially after dark.
- **After diving.** Give yourself time to rest after your last dive before the drive back.

## By bus: the budget option

If you prefer not to drive, buses run regularly from Metro Manila terminals to Batangas City. From Batangas City, local jeepneys continue toward Mabini, and tricycles cover the last few kilometres along the coast to Anilao.

The bus route takes longer than driving because of the connections, so plan for a full morning and travel light. Check current schedules with the bus operator before you go, as timetables change.

## By private transfer: the easy option

Many dive resorts and operators in Anilao can arrange a private van from Manila, which is often the most comfortable choice for groups, families and divers with gear. You are collected from your hotel or the airport and dropped at your door, with no tolls or parking to think about.

If you are coming to see a property, ask our team when you arrange your visit and we can advise on the best way to reach the site.

## When to go

Anilao is a year-round destination, but **peak visibility runs from November to May**, with water at about 26–30 °C. The rainy season, from June to October, brings more changeable weather; many divers still come for the macro life on sheltered sites. Read more in our [Anilao guide](/anilao#getting-there).

Weekdays are quieter on the road and on the reef. If you can only travel on a weekend, an early start makes the biggest difference.

## Arriving at The Coral

The Coral at Anilao sits on a hillside **60 to 80 metres above the sea**, about **two minutes (637 m) from the Mabini Circumferential Road**. From the upper slopes the view runs west to Sombrero and Tingloy Islands, with the sun setting over the water.

Parking is planned at the edge of the estate, so the paths between homes stay quiet and walkable. Site visits are by appointment; see [The Coral at Anilao](/destinations/the-coral-anilao) for the homes, the masterplan and our contact details.

## A quick checklist

- Plan for about two hours from NAIA by car, longer on holiday weekends.
- Load your toll account and leave early.
- Pack for sun and the occasional rain shower, plus reef-safe sunscreen.
- Book dives with a local dive centre; guided dives are the norm.
- For a site visit to The Coral or Ocean View, contact our team in advance.

However you travel, the reward is the same: one of the richest stretches of reef in the Philippines, within reach of a morning’s drive.
